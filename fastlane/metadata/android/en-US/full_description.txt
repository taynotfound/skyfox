SkyFox is a free, open-source flight tracker for Android built for aviation enthusiasts and plane spotters.

Track nearby aircraft live on an interactive map. Search by callsign, registration, HEX or SQUAWK. Build watchlists and receive alerts for specific aircraft, callsigns, squawk codes or locations.

Features:
• Live aircraft map with selectable map layers
• Search by HEX/ICAO, callsign, registration, airframe or SQUAWK
• Route information with origin and destination airports
• Aircraft, callsign, SQUAWK and location alerts
• Emergency squawk alerts (7500, 7600, 7700)
• Feeder monitoring with offline notifications
• Aircraft photos where available
• Watchlists, spotting statistics and shareable summaries
• No account required. No ads.

Aircraft data is provided by airplanes.live. SkyFox is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by airplanes.live.

SkyFox is free, open source, and licensed under GPL-3.0.
